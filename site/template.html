Is the monitor working?

Win rate is the share of a member's purchases that beat SPY over days. Members with fewer than scored trades are excluded — below that the number is noise.

Open-market purchases by Section 16 insiders. Rows marked with an accent bar are tickers Congress also bought in the same window — the overlap that produces a cross-signal.

Largest insider buys

Price against SPY since the purchase, both indexed to 100.

Every alert is scored twice: from the politician's trade date, and from the day the alert actually reached you. Only the second says whether this tool is worth running — disclosure lag means the first includes returns you had no way to capture. Both are measured against a control group of congressional trades that never triggered an alert.
