Every alert is scored twice: from the politician's trade date, and
      from the day the alert actually reached you. Only the second says whether this
      tool is worth running — disclosure lag means the first includes returns you had
      no way to capture. Both are measured against a control group of congressional
      trades that never triggered an alert.
